About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking webpage for speed and ease of remembering important information. It helps users capture and organize ideas before they are forgotten.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. create a new note
  2. write down your important information
  3. submit your note

Features

  • easy to use
  • simple interface
  • fast and efficient

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
ctr+n create a new note
ctr+s save your note
ctr+d delete your note
